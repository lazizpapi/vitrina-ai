## What to say when asked "why not just use Higgsfield directly"

Three answers, in this order.

A seller in Uzbekistan cannot pay a US API with an Uzcard or Humo card. Vitrina
takes soʻm through Click and gives the seller a balance.

A seller does not write English prompts. They type a product name and three
selling points in the language they speak, and the prompt writing happens
underneath.

A seller does not know marketplace photo rules. The main card is built to pass
moderation, and the output is already at the exact spec the platform wants.
